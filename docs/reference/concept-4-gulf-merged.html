Why the bottom of the chain defaults, even though it's owed money

A material supplier isn't only a lender. It's also a borrower: it buys raw materials on credit, pays wages and rent every month, and draws on a bank working-capital line. When its buyers pay late, the cash it was counting on doesn't arrive, but its own bills still fall due.

  1. It gets paid lateEmirates Steel ships SAR 750M of steel. Its Tier 2 buyers, squeezed from above, stretch payment toward 90 days.
  2. Its cash runs shortIts own costs keep coming: scrap and billets bought on 30-day terms, payroll, interest on the bank line.
  3. It starts paying its own creditors lateTo survive, it delays its suppliers and its bank. Now it is the late-paying buyer.
  4. It defaultsIt can no longer meet what it owes. On this map, "default" means a company has stopped paying its own debts, not that it went unpaid.

How the network spots it

  • Money coming in slows. Tags where the company is the supplier show its buyers paying later and later. That's the early warning.
  • Money going out slows. Tags where the company is the buyer show it paying its own suppliers late. That's the decisive signal.
  • A default is reported. When one supplier marks a line as defaulted, every other connected supplier gets a network alert.
  • It has nowhere else to turn. Shared suppliers like Emirates Steel and National Tube depend on two buyers. When both slow down at once, there's no other income.

FAQ

Common questions.

Everything you need to know about TagMyCredit, trade credit scoring, and the network.

What is TagMyCredit?

The world's first B2B Trade Credit Intelligence Network. Suppliers tag the credit they give buyers, buyers confirm, and everyone gets risk scores and alerts built from the whole network.

How does the TMC-ID work?

Every registered company gets a unique, permanent ID such as TMC-OM-8397-4521-01-K: prefix, country, tax-ID fragment, registration fragment, sequence and check digit. It is anonymous to other members: your company details are never shown on the network.

What is AAI (Anonymous Aggregated Intelligence)?

The privacy model. You see combined signals about a buyer, such as a score, an exposure range and payment behaviour, never which suppliers are involved or their exact amounts.

Who can see my trade credit data?

Only you see your own credit lines in full. The network sees aggregated intelligence; competitors never see your receivables, balances, statements or transactions.

How is the Trade Risk Score calculated?

An 8-component score from 0 to 1000, including payment discipline, utilization, bad debt history and financial strength, updated as tags and payments change.

Is TagMyCredit available in my country?

The network starts in the GCC, with Saudi Arabia and the UAE first.

What does it cost?

The Starter plan is free forever. No credit card required.

How long does setup take?

About 3 minutes to register and get your TMC-ID, then you can tag your first credit line.
